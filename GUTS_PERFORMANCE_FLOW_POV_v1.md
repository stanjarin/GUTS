# GUTS — PERFORMANCE FLOW POV v1
## Shed summary — 22 September 2026

This is the performance as experienced by the two people in the room. It is deliberately **POV, not engineering architecture**. Preserve that distinction.

## Sp POV

1. P introduces the improbability premise.
2. Sp writes an improbable sentence.
3. P tells the H2G2 / improbability yarn.
4. P apparently searches H2 and reads the relevant “HD Improb” result.
5. Sp types the short URL supplied by P.
6. Sp reaches the Resources gateway and chooses freely from the offered routes.
7. GUTS presents the library/book choices; Sp chooses.
8. Sp selects a cover, enters Contents, chooses a chapter and flips pages naturally.
9. Sp encounters the improbable word in the book.
10. The rest of the books/pages remain clean.
11. Later, Sp goes home and may check browser history / revisit the route.
12. The route now resolves harmlessly to the real Project Gutenberg.
13. Nothing remains for Sp to rediscover. Eventually, Sp forgets the bloody trick.

## P POV

### Pre-show

1. Activate **ARM authorisation** as required.
2. Put GUTS into **SHOW** before performance.

### Performance

3. Introduce Sp and the improbability premise.
4. Have Sp write the improbable sentence.
5. Perform the H2G2 story/search sequence.
6. H2 SEARCH covertly **ARMs** the selected word in GUTS.
7. Brief Sp only as much as necessary:
   - give the short URL;
   - let Sp choose at the Resources gateway;
   - **GUTS supplies the book choices and everything downstream.**
8. **Butt out.** Once Sp is inside GUTS, stop helping unless genuinely necessary.
9. Enjoy the momentary faint amusement while Sp independently discovers the impossible word.

### After the effect

10. Surreptitiously restore GUTS to **REHEARSAL**.
11. From then on, ordinary Sp revisits/history routes resolve to the real Project Gutenberg.
12. Rehearse at home until the mechanics disappear from P's performance.

## Governing principle — LEAVE NO TRACE

> **Leave it as you found it.**

Before the effect: **real Gutenberg is there.**

During the effect: **GUTS temporarily bends the environment.**

After the effect: **real Gutenberg is there again.**

This is more than conventional “end clean.” The temporary performance environment itself cleans up. The spectator should be left with no special app, prepared page, revealing route, or persistent constructed library to rediscover.

Any future GUTS feature should be tested against this principle: **does it improve the effect without leaving unnecessary traces afterward?**
