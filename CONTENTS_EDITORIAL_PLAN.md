# GUTS Contents Editorial Plan — Bench Test

## Governing rule
Every visible/selectable Contents destination must be performance-capable. No clean/dead chapter choices.

The 0.35 performance corpus remains authoritative for eligible destinations. Do not restore cut chapters merely for bibliographic completeness. Instead, regularise the spectator-facing Contents so retained destinations form a plausible edition.

## Display policy
1. Keep underlying book/chapter IDs and WORD sockets unchanged.
2. Renumber retained chapters consecutively for display where engineering cuts created gaps.
3. Prefer genuine source chapter/subtitles where useful and available.
4. Where the source genuinely has only chapter numbers, plain sequential chapter labels are acceptable; invented descriptive headings are optional editorial dressing, not required.
5. Remove malformed extraction fragments from headings.
6. Never expose a selectable destination that cannot pay off.
